The honest version

Canada created the credit system. Grizzl-E runs the Club.

Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations create a market for clean-fuel credits. Eligible EV charging can help generate those credits.

Grizzl-E Club is a private membership program administered by United Chargers Inc. It collects eligible charging data, obtains Clean Fuel Credits and shares value with members as cash rewards. The Government of Canada does not approve memberships, supply chargers or guarantee payments.

Questions people ask

Clear answers before you share your email.

Is this a Government of Canada charger rebate?

No. Grizzl-E Club is a private program run by United Chargers. Its cash-reward model uses Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations credit system, but the federal government does not approve members, supply chargers or guarantee Club rewards.

Is the charger really free?

Approved members pay no hardware purchase price for a supplied Club charger. You still pay the applicable refundable security deposit, shipping and professional installation costs. During an active supplied-charger membership, the charger remains United Chargers property and must be returned if membership ends.

How do the cash rewards work?

Rewards accrue after eligible charging sessions based on successfully recorded kWh and the member's current tier. Current published tiers range from 3¢ to 10¢ per kWh. Missing data caused by connectivity or system issues is not necessarily credited later.

What does the referring member receive?

If you register, activate a qualifying charger and remain eligible, the referring Club member can earn CAD $0.01 for each eligible kWh your charger records. This does not reduce your own Club reward. You receive no guaranteed extra benefit solely because you were referred.

What happens after I submit?

Your request and consent are recorded. The referring member uses your email in the official Grizzl-E Club referral tool. United Chargers sends the invitation and independently decides your application, charger and program eligibility.

How long is an invitation valid?

Current referral terms say invitations, links or codes may expire 15 days after issuance and may require all program steps to be completed within the applicable window. Act promptly and follow the official invitation instructions.
