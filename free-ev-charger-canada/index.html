Free EV charger in Canada? Price the conditions, not just the hardware.

Current private programs can put a Level 2 charger in an eligible Canadian home with no upfront hardware purchase price. That does not erase the deposit, shipping, electrical work, Wi-Fi, data, usage, ownership, reward or exit terms.

Quick answer

Yes, offers exist. No, “free” is not one standard deal.

Grizzl-E Club is a supplied-hardware membership path: an approved member can receive a charger with no hardware purchase price, while United Chargers retains ownership during a supplied-charger membership. Deposit, shipping, installation, Wi-Fi, active primary use, charging data and return conditions apply under current terms.[1]

SWTCH Home Charging Program is an ownership path: its current terms say one charger per residential property can be acquired with no upfront hardware cost and ownership transfers to the customer on delivery. A $300 CAD deposit, shipping, installation, SWTCH-network connectivity, incentive assignment, reward and cancellation conditions still apply.[2]

Provincial and utility incentives are different again. Natural Resources Canada says rebates covering up to 50% of installation cost are available in some provinces, and warns that some require pre-approval before work begins.[4]

Government framework, private offers

Clean Fuel Regulations are not a government charger rebate.

The federal Clean Fuel Regulations establish a credit market. Charging-network operators can create credits for residential EV charging, and revenues from residential and public charging credits must be reinvested in charging infrastructure or consumer financial incentives.[5]

That framework does not make these provider offers government-operated. Grizzl-E Club and the SWTCH Home Charging Program set their own applications, equipment, deposits, connectivity, rewards and termination terms. Neither provider’s offer is a Government of Canada approval or entitlement.

Common questions

Four definitions worth keeping.

Can you get a free EV charger in Canada?

Some private programs offer eligible participants a charger with no upfront hardware purchase price. Deposits, shipping, electrician work, connectivity, data, usage, rewards and exit conditions can still apply. Government or utility rebates are separate.

Is this a Government of Canada charger rebate?

No. The private programs compared here use a market created by the Clean Fuel Regulations, but the providers—not the Government of Canada—set applications, equipment, rewards and program terms.

What can still cost money?

Depending on the offer and your home: refundable deposit, shipping, electrician labour, permit, new circuit, load management, panel upgrade, trenching, removal, restocking and return shipping.

Which program is best?

No single program is best for every household. Compare approval, total installation cost, ownership, app and Wi-Fi requirements, carbon-credit rights, reward variability and cancellation terms against your situation.
