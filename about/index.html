Editorial standards

Useful first. Verifiable always.

  1. 01
    Start with primary evidence.

    Material claims are checked against current first-party terms, provider pages and government sources. Search snippets and summaries can help find a source, but they do not replace reading it.

  2. 02
    Date volatile claims.

    Each factual guide shows when its volatile claims were checked. Rates, eligibility, compatibility and program rules can change, so readers are sent to the current official source before acting.

  3. 03
    Separate evidence from interpretation.

    When provider copy and legal terms differ in detail, legal terms take priority. The site does not claim a hands-on hardware test unless one was actually performed.

  4. 04
    State the limits beside the claim.

    The site does not turn a private reward program into a government rebate, endorsement or guaranteed entitlement. Deposits, shipping, installation, ownership, connectivity, data and exit conditions are treated as decision facts.

  5. 05
    Keep commercial influence visible.

    Material commercial relationships are disclosed beside the decisions they may affect. The site identifies when Peter may receive referral compensation and when it receives no compensation from a compared provider.
