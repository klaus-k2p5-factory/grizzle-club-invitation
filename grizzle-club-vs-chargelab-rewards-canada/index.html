Grizzl-E Club vs ChargeLab Rewards: which program fits?

The shortest answer is about hardware: Grizzl-E Club supplies an eligible charger under Club conditions; ChargeLab Rewards is built for an eligible Canadian homeowner who already owns a compatible smart charger. The ownership, app, payout and exit details matter as much as the headline reward rate.

Compare the terms Review the Grizzl-E invitation

This is not a hands-on review and does not name one program “best.” It compares current published terms. Provider terms can change and always control.

Start here
Need eligible hardware?

Grizzl-E Club is the relevant path to examine—after pricing deposit, shipping, installation, connectivity and ownership conditions.

Already own a compatible charger?

ChargeLab may be the simpler path—after confirming single-family eligibility, OCPP compatibility, app migration and credit exclusivity.

Neither description fits?

A conventional charger purchase or utility rebate may be a better option than either rewards program.

Quick answer

Choose by hardware and obligations—not a single cents-per-kWh number.

Grizzl-E Club is the more relevant option to investigate if you want access to a supplied Grizzl-E charger and can accept a refundable deposit, shipping, installation, continuous Wi-Fi, active primary-use requirements and United Chargers ownership of a supplied charger during membership.

ChargeLab Rewards is the more relevant option to investigate if you already own an eligible OCPP 1.6-or-newer smart charger at a Canadian single-family residence and are willing to connect it to ChargeLab, grant ChargeLab exclusive rights to claim the charger’s carbon credits during enrolment, and receive rewards under its current quarterly payout rules.

Supplied-hardware path

Grizzl-E Club

A connected Club charger, a tiered cash-reward schedule and more responsibility than “free charger” implies.

What stands out

  • No supplied-charger hardware purchase price for an approved member.
  • Current reward tiers begin at 3¢/kWh and rise only for future sessions after cumulative thresholds.
  • The current security deposit is described as refundable after the first eligible online charging session; the amount depends on charger and enrolment choice.
  • The supplied charger remains United Chargers property during membership.

What can disqualify the fit

  • You cannot maintain Wi-Fi at the installation location.
  • You want unconditional ownership of supplied hardware.
  • You intend to use the charger only as an occasional backup.
  • You cannot cover shipping, permits, electrical work or installation when required.
  • You are uncomfortable with connected-session data and Clean Fuel Credit participation.

Existing-hardware path

ChargeLab Rewards

A current flat-rate reward for an eligible single-family homeowner who already owns compatible connected hardware.

What stands out

  • Current published rate of 10¢/qualifying residential kWh.
  • No subscription or registration fee under the current provider page and terms.
  • Quarterly rewards; current terms describe digital gift cards or another delivery method selected by ChargeLab.
  • Current examples include Autel, Siemens, Wallbox, SWTCH/Home EVL007 and Grizzl-E Smart, subject to compatibility verification.

What can disqualify the fit

  • Your home is multi-tenant, shared-use, commercial or fleet charging.
  • Your charger is not compatible or cannot remain connected.
  • You need to keep another platform claiming carbon credits for the same charger.
  • You do not want to migrate a single-connection charger to the ChargeLab app.
  • You expect a fixed rate or permanent program; the terms allow changes.

Often missed

Seven questions to answer before you enrol.

  1. 01
    Who supplies and owns the charger?

    Separate a hardware benefit from ownership. Confirm what happens on cancellation and full program termination.

  2. 02
    What will the electrical work cost?

    Neither headline reward eliminates panel, circuit, permit or electrician costs.

  3. 03
    Can the charger stay online?

    Missed or disconnected sessions may not be recoverable, and both programs rely on transmitted charging data.

  4. 04
    Which app controls charging?

    Check scheduling and whether your charger can keep its manufacturer app connection.

  5. 05
    How are rewards delivered?

    Cash/EFT, gift card, payout threshold and payment frequency are materially different.

  6. 06
    Who can claim the carbon credits?

    Do not enrol one charger in conflicting programs that claim the same charging activity.

  7. 07
    What if the program or rate changes?

    Read modification, notice, final-payout, hardware return and ownership-transfer clauses—not only today’s rate.

Before the form

A ten-minute fit check can prevent an expensive mismatch.

  • Confirm Canadian residence, age and property type.
  • Identify your charger model—or whether you need one supplied.
  • Confirm plug, amperage, panel and circuit feasibility.
  • Check Wi-Fi at the parking location.
  • Price deposit, shipping, installation and permits.
  • Read current reward, payout and termination clauses.
  • Confirm which app and carbon-credit operator will control the charger.
