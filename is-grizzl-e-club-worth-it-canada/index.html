Is Grizzl-E Club worth it? Check the fit, not just the headline.

The Club can be appealing if you need connected home-charging hardware and accept its ongoing conditions. It can be a poor match if installation, Wi-Fi, active-use, data, ownership or return obligations do not fit your home.

Run the six-part check Review the invitation path

This is a terms-based decision guide, not a hands-on product review, membership decision or provider rating. Current official terms always control.

Thirty-second answer
Likely worth investigating

You need eligible hardware, can cover setup costs, have stable Wi-Fi and will make it your regular home charger.

Probably not a fit

You want unconditional hardware ownership, occasional backup use, an offline charger or a predictable fixed reward.

Do not decide from “up to 15¢” alone

That headline combines an Ultimate base tier with a future, eligibility-limited bonus-points offer.

Short answer

It can fit—but only after the practical conditions make sense.

Look closer if: you are an eligible Canadian EV driver with dedicated home parking, need a connected charger, can pay shipping and any required electrical work, can keep the charger online, and expect to use it as your primary charger.

Skip or compare other paths if: you already own compatible hardware, cannot maintain Wi-Fi, charge only occasionally, dislike the data arrangement, or want a charger you own without return conditions. The independent program comparison covers an existing-hardware alternative.

Evidence window: official Club terms version June 10, 2026; official bonus announcement published August 12, 2026; checked August 14, 2026.
New headline, important limits

What “up to 15¢/kWh” actually means.

The official announcement is useful news, but it does not mean every member earns 15¢ today. The current base cash-reward tiers remain 3¢–10¢ per eligible kWh; the promotional 5¢ component has separate timing and eligibility.

Six-part fit check

Answer every question before requesting an invitation.

A “no” does not make the Club bad. It means a conventional charger, a program for hardware you already own, or no rewards program may fit better.

01

Home parking in Canada

Do you have a Canadian installation location and the right to install or use the charger there? A non-owner may need prior written property-owner consent under the terms.

Good sign: dedicated home parking and clear permission.
02

Electrical work and installation budget

Can you pay for a code-compliant circuit, permit, electrician, panel work or receptacle changes when needed? The hardware offer does not cover these costs.

Good sign: an electrical assessment or realistic quote.
03

Reliable Wi-Fi at the parking location

Can the charger remain connected? The terms permit ten (10) additional charging sessions after Wi-Fi loss, then say charging is disabled until reconnection.

Good sign: stable 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi where the charger mounts.
04

Primary, active charging use

Will this be your regular home charger? Current terms define active use for Club status as a minimum of 4–6 charging sessions per month.

Good sign: regular overnight home charging, not backup storage.
05

Charging data and Clean Fuel Credits

Are you comfortable with charging-session data being collected and shared for Clean Fuel Credit participation? The terms say anonymization is used where possible but complete anonymity is not assured.

Good sign: you have read both terms and privacy information.
06

Ownership, cancellation and return

Can you accept that a supplied charger remains United Chargers property during membership and may need to be returned if membership ends, with return shipping and related costs governed by current terms?

Good sign: the exit path is acceptable before installation.
The real first-year decision

“No hardware purchase price” is not “no money due.”

Separate what is paid, what may be refunded, and what remains your responsibility.

Build a ballpark estimate
Cash due around setup
  • Refundable security deposit set by the current enrolment path
  • Shipping, handling and delivery charges
  • Electrical assessment, installation, permits and upgrades
Ongoing conditions
  • Reliable Wi-Fi and recorded charging sessions
  • Primary, active charger use
  • Compliance with changing Club and reward terms
Not the same as cash in hand
  • A refundable deposit is cash tied up until its condition is met
  • A future promotional point is not today’s cash withdrawal
  • A displayed referral or charging balance can remain subject to eligibility and review
Read before installation

The exit terms matter as much as the entry offer.

  1. 01
    During an active supplied-charger membership

    The charger remains United Chargers property. Ending membership does not automatically give the member ownership under the general supplied-charger terms.

  2. 02
    If membership ends

    The supplied charger must be returned under the applicable process and timeframes. Current terms assign outbound and return shipping costs to the member except where law or another provision applies.

  3. 03
    If the entire Club program ends

    The June 10 terms describe special treatment based on whether a supplied charger has been used for at least 36 months, including transfer, prorated purchase or return paths.

  4. 04
    If charging data is missed

    Sessions not captured because of connectivity or technical failures are not necessarily reconstructed for cash rewards or tier progress.

  5. 05
    If the offer changes

    Rates, promotional eligibility and program structure can change. Re-check the official terms and offer before ordering, installing or relying on future rewards.

Common decision questions

Three points to remember.

Is Grizzl-E Club worth it?

It may be worth investigating if you need eligible hardware and all six practical conditions on this page fit. It is probably not a fit if you need offline or occasional use, unconditional ownership, or cannot cover setup costs.

Does every member earn 15¢ per kWh now?

No. The August 12 announcement describes an additional 5¢ in bonus points beginning October 1, 2026 for eligible members who own their Grizzl-E charger. The 15¢ total requires the Ultimate level, and the extra points are described as converting to cash on October 1, 2027.

Who owns a supplied Club charger?

Under the terms checked August 14, 2026, a supplied charger remains United Chargers property during membership and must be returned if membership ends, subject to the applicable terms and program-end provisions.
