Know the conditions

Rewards depend on an active, connected Club membership.

Approval and current terms control. If you choose a supplied-charger path, its hardware and return conditions also apply.

Approval and Club path

United Chargers decides whether to approve your application, eligible charger path and program participation.

Wi-Fi and connected data

Keep the charger connected to Wi-Fi and follow the Club’s connected-charger requirements.

Active use

Active use is defined as a minimum of 4 to 6 charging sessions per month.

Supplied charger setup

If you choose supplied hardware, a refundable security deposit, shipping, handling, delivery, installation and electrical work can apply.

Ownership and exit

A supplied charger remains United Chargers property during membership. You must return the charger if membership ends under the current return terms.

Before you request

Straight answers about current rewards.

Are these a government rebate?

No. These are Grizzl-E Club rewards supported by Canada’s Clean Fuel Regulations credit system. It is not a direct government rebate or government endorsement; the Government of Canada does not approve applications, provide a Club charger or guarantee a payment.

What does “up to 10¢/kWh” mean?

The Club’s current published levels show rewards from 3¢/kWh to a maximum of 10¢/kWh. Your reward level and participation depend on the current Club path and terms.

Where can I review the official terms?

Read the official Club terms, then return here before registering so you can request the invitation first.
