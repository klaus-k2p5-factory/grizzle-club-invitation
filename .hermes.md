## User’s intent and copy preferences

- The goal is legitimate inbound referrals, not mass unsolicited outreach.
- The visitor requests an invitation; the operator then uses the official Grizzl-E Club referral tool to send it.
- Be persuasive, modern and clear without disguising the operator’s material referral interest.
- Keep material obligations visible before the form: deposit, shipping, professional installation/electrical work, Wi-Fi/data requirements, membership approval, charger ownership and return conditions.
- Keep the current active-primary-use obligation visible; the June 10, 2026 terms define active use as 4–6 charging sessions per month.
- Explain Canada’s Clean Fuel Regulations as the credit-system background. Never call the Club benefit a direct Government of Canada rebate, government-funded charger, government payment, approval or endorsement.
- Disclose that the referring member can earn `CAD $0.01 per eligible kWh` from a qualifying referred charger, subject to current terms.
- **Do not reintroduce copy saying “you receive no guaranteed referral-only bonus,” “no guaranteed benefit merely for being referred,” or equivalent.** The user considers it unnecessary and confusing because it can sound as though approved members will not receive ordinary Club charging rewards.
- When needed, say instead: `Approved members’ standard Club charging rewards are determined under current terms.`
- Do not guarantee approval, charger availability, rewards, a zero-cost installation or unconditional charger ownership.
- Do not copy official Grizzl-E or Government of Canada branding.
