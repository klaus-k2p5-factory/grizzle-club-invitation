The simple version

Free charger hardware is not the same as free home-charger setup.

The current Club page presents a supplied-hardware option. A Club charger is part of the membership arrangement, and the official application decides whether the option is available to you.

Requesting here does not enroll you, order a charger or guarantee approval. It starts the invitation-first path so the official invitation can be sent before you apply.

Know the conditions

Review these conditions before requesting an invitation.

United Chargers decides whether to approve your application. Current conditions can change, and the official terms always control.

Refundable security deposit

The applicable deposit and refund condition depend on the enrollment path and current terms. Do not treat the deposit as a charger purchase.

Shipping, handling and delivery

You cover these charges for a supplied charger.

Installation and electrical work

You cover any assessment, electrician, permit, receptacle, panel or other home electrical work needed for installation.

Wi-Fi and regular use

Keep the charger connected to Wi-Fi. Active use is defined as a minimum of 4 to 6 charging sessions per month.

Club ownership and return

A supplied charger remains United Chargers property during membership. You must return the charger if membership ends under the current return terms.

Before you request

Straight answers about the supplied-charger path.

Is the charger hardware really free?

The supplied-hardware path can have no charger purchase price for an approved member. A refundable deposit, shipping and installation can still apply, and the charger remains part of the Club arrangement.

Is this a government charger rebate?

No. A Club benefit supported by Canada’s Clean Fuel Regulations credit system. It is not a direct government rebate or government endorsement; United Chargers decides Club applications, supplied chargers and rewards under current terms.

Where can I review the official terms?

Read the official Club terms, then return here before registering so you can request the invitation first.
